GOT QUESTIONS?

A Few Helpful Answers

Here are some things to consider before welcoming a cat home.

How much does it cost to adopt a cat?

Fees vary by organization and location. Ask the shelter what is included, such as veterinary care, vaccinations, or microchipping.

Can I adopt a cat if I live in an apartment?

Many cats can live happily in apartments if their needs are met. Consider enrichment, safe windows, scratching surfaces, and housing rules.

Can a cat live with children or other pets?

It depends on the individual cat and household. Ask about known behavior, introduce pets gradually, and supervise interactions with children.

How long does the adoption process take?

Every organization has its own process. It may include an application, conversation, meeting, and home or lifestyle checks where appropriate.

What should I prepare before bringing a cat home?

Prepare food, water bowls, a litter tray, scratching post, hiding places, toys, a secure carrier, and a plan for veterinary care.
